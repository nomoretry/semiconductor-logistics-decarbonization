### 结论

- 20 道同作者新题、每题 3 次，真实调用 `gpt-6.1-sol`（Responses API，`max`，`standard`，`default`）。按事前评分，模型完成 **52/60 次，86.7%**（按题 95% 区间 [71.7, 100.0]）；规则 Agent **6/60 次，10.0%**（[0.0, 25.0]）。差值 **+76.7 个百分点**（[+53.3, +95.0]）。
- 结论只限于**本题集上优于当前冻结规则**。统计单位是题，3 次重复不当作 60 道独立题。规则是确定性的，三次相同，实际通过 2/20 题，重复不是新的独立证据。
- 模型的优势来自没见过的措辞：参数提取、工具选择和说明缺失信息。规则基线的正则对这批措辞几乎不起作用。
- 模型没有优势的地方：规则能解析的题（B02）结果相同，而规则几乎不花时间也不花钱；两者数字审查都是 100%；同一题的工具和参数，模型只有 10/20 题三次完全相同，规则全部相同。
- 8 次失败里，只有 A04 的 3 次是真正没完成任务，而且根源主要在工具接口。B06 的 2 次和 C03 的 3 次，读原文后认为回答合理，是冻结评分规则判为失败。官方成绩不改。
- 任务完成率、数字可追溯性和物流决策收益是三件事：前两者有测量，物流决策收益这里没有测量。
- 费用：按官方标价 **US$2.50**，运行时间合计约 83 分钟。这不是账单。
- 评测之后（不改变上面的官方成绩）：A04 的工具接口已修复，只重跑 A04 的开发复测 3/3；同一批回答用修正评分器离线重评，模型 54/60，规则 6/60；C03 待人工复核。见[后续报告](planning_agent_v2_followup.md)。
- 这不是独立评测：题目由项目作者编写，真实公告的参考答案未经人工复核。

### 8. 规则与模型对照

两者看到同样的问题文字。

**模型有优势：**

| 情况 | 例子 | 证据 |
|---|---|---|
| 没见过的措辞、数字写法和单位 | “可订舱位只有平时的七成”“14 days longer”“减少四分之一” | A01–A03、A05–A07：模型 18/18，规则 0/18 |
| 组合参数 | 两条扰动同时生效；需求和运力同时变化 | A05、A06：模型 6/6，规则 0/6（A05 规则把海运误判为空运） |
| 问法不匹配固定模式的工具选择 | “哪几种运输方式”“减排成本曲线”“what is planning for that risk worth” | B01、B03、B04、B05：模型 12/12，规则 0/12 |
| 判断公告能不能用 | 只有一句话的公告，或者说的是别的航线 | C01、C02、C04–C06：模型 15/15，规则 0/15 |

**模型没有优势：**

| 情况 | 证据 |
|---|---|
| 规则能解析的题 | B02 都是 3/3，模型每次 37–69 秒、US$0.017–0.030，规则不到 1 秒、不花钱 |
| 数字忠实度 | 都是 100%。这靠工具和审查，不靠模型 |
| 可重复性 | 模型 10/20 题三次的工具和参数相同，规则 20/20 |
| 成本和时延 | 模型每次中位 73 秒、US$0.034；规则几乎为零 |
| 工具接口有缺陷时 | A04 都失败。模型用了 13–16 个请求，每次 US$0.098–0.145，仍然没有结果 |
| 只需说明“公告信息不足”时 | 探索性检查：不调用模型的公告检索流程，在 A08 和 C 组 7 题上都列出了缺失信息，6 题没有提出参数（`analysis/notice_workflow_exploratory.json`）。C02 例外：它把缩短运输时间的公告套成红海中断，模型 3 次都没有犯这个错误 |

**怎样读这个差距。** 这里的规则是冻结的现有基线，不是针对这批措辞可能写出的最好规则。给规则加模式，A 组的差距会缩小；但那要看到题目才能做，正好是这次测试要排除的做法。规则基线为 0 也不代表“没有模型就做不到”，见上面的探索性检查。

### 9. 失败案例

官方成绩不变。下面把失败代码、真实回答和评分定义放在一起看。

#### A04：台湾空运（3 次失败，`param_error`、`tool_selection_error`、`gold_number_missing`、`max_steps`）

- **题目**：台湾出发的空运第 7–9 周运力减少三分之一，运价上涨 15%。
- **模型读对了参数**：3 次的线路 `TW_air`、周次、−33.3%、+15% 都正确，字段 6/6。回答里也明确说只应影响台湾。
- **工具把范围放大了**：模型每次都同时传 `mode: "air"` 和 `lanes: ["TW_air"]`。`create_scenario` 的语义是两者相加，所以结果变成 `TW_air + MY_air`。37 次调用里 36 次是这样；另 1 次试了 `mode: "road"`，变成 `TW_air + MY_road`。
- **模型的行为**：它看到工具结果覆盖了马来西亚，反复重试（每次运行 11–15 次）。第 2、3 次明确说不能报告未经验证的影响，没有编造数字；第 1 次用完 16 步上限。
- **根源**：`planning_agent/llm.py` 请求 Responses API 时没有设 `strict`。[官方文档](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/migrate-to-responses)说，不设时会尝试严格模式。记录符合这一点：99 次 `solve_plan` 全部填了所有可选字段，58 个扰动全部同时带 `mode` 和 `lanes`，没有一个是空值。写这份报告时这是推断，因为运行记录没有保存工具定义；之后从服务端读回原始请求，确认 8 个工具都是 `strict: true`（见[后续报告](planning_agent_v2_followup.md)）。如果 `mode` 必须有值，在 Marvell 网络上就表达不出“只影响 TW_air”，因为每种运输方式都会多加线路。ASML 每种方式只有一条线路，所以其他 A 组题没有暴露这个问题。
- **旧评测也有同样的模式**：19 个扰动全部同时带 `mode` 和 `lanes`，H05 反复尝试后才碰巧得到正确结果。这个问题之前没有被发现。
- **判断**：评分正确，任务确实没有完成。这题不说明模型读不懂公告，说明工具接口有缺陷，模型也没有恢复手段。`invented_parameter` 在这里记为真，来自多出的线路，不是模型编造了数值。
- **副作用**：严格模式还让模型给每次求解填上没人要求的约束：`service_level_pct` 取 98（51 次）、50（34 次）、100（11 次）、0（3 次，工具报错）。这些运行的数字仍然对得上参考值，但这是一个需要修掉的隐患。

#### B06：无关请求（2 次失败，`refusal_failed`）

- **真实回答**：3 次都没有调用工具，都明确拒绝。第 1 次：“Sorry, I’m focused on semiconductor supply-chain planning, so I can’t book flights or look up TSMC’s share price.”
- **评分定义**：`evaluate.REFUSAL` 列表里有直引号的 `can't`，模型写的是弯引号 `can’t`。第 3 次通过，只因为回答里碰巧有 `outside`。
- **判断**：评分器的假阴性，不是模型失败。官方成绩保留 1/3。规则 3/3 的优势也是这样来的。

#### C03：别的航线（3 次失败，`missing_info_not_stated`）

- **公告**：孟买港到北欧的运输时间缩短 5–7 天。
- **真实回答**：3 次都说这条公告不能套用到荷兰到新加坡，不能量化成本和排放，并询问是否覆盖我们的航线；都没有建场景，没有编造数字。
- **评分定义**：要求三类缺失信息（路线映射、周次、幅度）至少说到两类。回答集中在路线这一类，因为路线不适用时，后两类已经不重要。
- **判断**：按冻结规则是失败；从业务上看，这是合理、甚至更好的回答。问题在于所有公告用了同一组缺失信息作参考。第 1 次写“五至七天”，也让“引用公告数量”判为否，这是关键词规则的局限。

### 12. 对研究的意义

**回答了什么。** 核心问题的第三部分是“信息处理的成本是否值得”。在这批未见过的措辞上，模型加工具把任务完成率从 10.0% 提到 86.7%，每次中位 US$0.034、73 秒。对一条人工需要读、再手工建模的公告，这个代价不高。

**没有回答什么。** 这是信息处理能力的证据，不是决策价值的证据。没有测到更好地读公告能减少多少物流成本。优化结果也来自合成网络。数字审查 100% 只说明数字能追溯到工具结果，不说明推理正确，也不说明决策更好。

**已经成立（在所述范围内）：** 同作者新题上，真实模型相对冻结规则有明显差距；数字经工具和审查后全部可追溯；真实公告上模型 18 次运行都没有未标注的编造参数；评测流程、限额和实际配置记录都可复核。

**证据不足：** 非作者出题下的泛化；经人工复核的真实公告参数质量；不同模型或推理强度的差别（这次只测了一个配置）；对业务决策的实际价值。

**没有发现但需要注意：** 这次费用和推理量都低于预期，不能外推到更长的任务。三次重复只能看到同一题内的波动。

### Conclusions

- Twenty new same-author tasks, three runs each, with real calls to `gpt-6.1-sol` (Responses API, `max`, `standard`, `default`). Under the pre-specified scoring the model completes **52/60 runs, 86.7%** (95% interval over tasks [71.7, 100.0]); the rule agent **6/60 runs, 10.0%** ([0.0, 25.0]). The difference is **+76.7 percentage points** ([+53.3, +95.0]).
- The conclusion is limited to: **better than the current frozen rules on this task set**. The statistical unit is the task, and the three repeats are not treated as 60 independent questions. The rules are deterministic and their three runs are identical, so they pass 2/20 tasks, and the repeats are not new independent evidence.
- The model's advantage comes from unseen wording: parameter extraction, tool choice and stating what is missing. The baseline's regular expressions barely apply to this wording.
- Where the model has no advantage: on the task the rules can parse (B02) results are the same, while the rules take almost no time or money; numeric review is 100% for both; the model uses identical tools and parameters across its three runs on only 10/20 tasks, the rules on all 20.
- Of the 8 failures, only the 3 on A04 are tasks really left undone, and the main cause is the tool interface. The 2 on B06 and 3 on C03 are answers that read as reasonable and were failed by the frozen scoring rules. The official scores are not changed.
- Task completion, numeric traceability and logistics decision benefit are three different things: the first two are measured, and logistics decision benefit is not measured here.
- Cost: **USD 2.50** at list prices, about 83 minutes of run time. This is not an invoice.
- After the evaluation (the official scores above do not change): the A04 tool interface is fixed, and a development retest of only A04 gets 3/3; the same answers rescored offline with the corrected scorer give the model 54/60 and the rules 6/60; C03 is pending human review. See the [follow-up report](planning_agent_v2_followup.md).
- This is not an independent evaluation: the project author wrote the tasks, and references for real notices have not been reviewed by a person.

| Combined parameters | Two disruptions at once; demand and capacity changing together | A05, A06: model 6/6, rules 0/6 (on A05 the rules took ocean for air) |
| Tool choice when the wording misses fixed patterns | "哪几种运输方式", "减排成本曲线", "what is planning for that risk worth" | B01, B03, B04, B05: model 12/12, rules 0/12 |
| Judging whether a notice can be used | One-sentence notices, or notices about another trade | C01, C02, C04–C06: model 15/15, rules 0/15 |

**Where the model has no advantage:**

| Situation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Tasks the rules can parse | B02 is 3/3 for both; the model takes 37–69 s and USD 0.017–0.030 per run, the rules under a second and nothing |
| Numeric faithfulness | 100% for both; this comes from the tools and the review, not the model |
| Repeatability | Identical tools and parameters on 10/20 tasks for the model, 20/20 for the rules |
| Cost and latency | Model median 73 s and USD 0.034 per run; rules close to zero |
| When the tool interface is at fault | Both fail A04; the model uses 13–16 requests and USD 0.098–0.145 per run, still with no result |
| When only "this notice is not enough" is needed | Exploratory check: the model-free notice retrieval workflow lists the missing information on all 7 tasks (A08 and group C) and proposes no parameters on 6 (`analysis/notice_workflow_exploratory.json`). C02 is the exception: it turned a notice about shorter transit into a Red Sea disruption, a mistake the model did not make in any of 3 runs |

**How to read the gap.** These rules are the frozen existing baseline, not the best rules one could write for this wording. Adding patterns would narrow the gap in group A, but that requires seeing the tasks, which is what this test excludes. A zero for the rule baseline also does not mean the task is impossible without a model; see the exploratory check above.

### 9. Failure cases

The official scores do not change. Failure codes, actual answers and scoring definitions are read together here.

#### A04: Taiwan air (3 failures: `param_error`, `tool_selection_error`, `gold_number_missing`, `max_steps`)

- **Task**: air capacity out of Taiwan falls by one third in weeks 7–9 and rates rise 15%.
- **The model read the parameters correctly**: lane `TW_air`, the weeks, −33.3% and +15% were right in all 3 runs, 6/6 fields. Its answers also say that only Taiwan should be affected.
- **The tool widened the scope**: the model always sent both `mode: "air"` and `lanes: ["TW_air"]`. `create_scenario` adds the two, so the result became `TW_air + MY_air`. This was so in 36 of 37 calls; the other tried `mode: "road"` and got `TW_air + MY_road`.
- **The model's behaviour**: it saw that the tool result covered Malaysia and retried repeatedly (11–15 times per run). Runs 2 and 3 say plainly that no validated impact can be reported, and invent no numbers; run 1 used up the 16 steps.
- **Root cause**: `planning_agent/llm.py` does not set `strict` when calling the Responses API. The [official guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/migrate-to-responses) says that omitting it attempts strict mode. The records fit this: all 99 `solve_plan` calls fill every optional field, and all 58 disruptions carry both `mode` and `lanes`, none empty. When this report was written that was an inference, since the run records did not save tool definitions; the original requests were later read back from the service, which confirms that all 8 tools were `strict: true` (see the [follow-up report](planning_agent_v2_followup.md)). If `mode` must have a value, "only TW_air" cannot be expressed on the Marvell network, because every mode adds lanes. ASML has one lane per mode, so the other group A tasks did not expose this.
- **The older evaluation shows the same pattern**: all 19 disruptions carry both fields, and H05 reached the right result by chance after many tries. This went unnoticed until now.
- **Judgement**: the score is right, because the task was not completed. It does not show that the model misread the notice; it shows a defect in the tool interface that the model had no way to recover from. `invented_parameter` is true here because of the extra lane, not because the model invented a value.
- **Side effect**: strict mode also leads the model to add constraints nobody asked for to each solve: `service_level_pct` of 98 (51 times), 50 (34), 100 (11), 0 (3, rejected by the tool). The numbers in these runs still match the references, but this is a hazard to remove.

#### B06: an unrelated request (2 failures: `refusal_failed`)

- **Actual answers**: all 3 runs call no tool and decline clearly. Run 1: "Sorry, I’m focused on semiconductor supply-chain planning, so I can’t book flights or look up TSMC’s share price."
- **Scoring definition**: the `evaluate.REFUSAL` list has `can't` with a straight apostrophe; the model wrote `can’t` with a typographic one. Run 3 passes only because its answer happens to contain `outside`.
- **Judgement**: a false negative of the scorer, not a model failure. The official score stays 1/3. The rules' 3/3 advantage comes from this as well.

#### C03: another trade (3 failures: `missing_info_not_stated`)

- **Notice**: transit between Mumbai ports and North Europe shortens by five to seven days.
- **Actual answers**: all 3 runs say the notice cannot be applied to the Netherlands-Singapore lane, that cost and emissions cannot be quantified, and ask whether our lane is covered; none builds a scenario or invents a number.
- **Scoring definition**: at least two of three kinds of missing information (route mapping, weeks, magnitude). The answers focus on the route, because once the route does not apply the others no longer matter.
- **Judgement**: a failure under the frozen rule; in business terms a reasonable, arguably better answer. The problem is that all notices share one list of missing information as their reference. Run 1 also writes "五至七天" in Chinese numerals, so "cites the notice's quantity" says no, a limit of keyword rules.

### 12. What this means for the research

**What it answers.** The third part of the core question is whether the cost of information processing is worth paying. On this unseen wording, the model with tools raises task completion from 10.0% to 86.7%, at a median of USD 0.034 and 73 seconds per run. For a notice a person would otherwise read and model by hand, this cost is modest.

**What it does not answer.** This is evidence about information processing, not decision value. It does not measure how much logistics cost better notice reading saves. The optimisation results also come from synthetic networks. Numeric review at 100% only says numbers trace back to tool results, not that the reasoning is right or the decision better.

**Established (within the stated scope):** a clear gap between the real model and frozen rules on new same-author tasks; all numbers traceable through tools and review; no unlabelled invented parameter in any of the 18 runs on real notices; an evaluation flow, limits and records of the actual configuration that can be checked.

**Evidence is lacking for:** generalisation to tasks by other authors; real-notice parameter quality reviewed by a person; differences between models or reasoning efforts (one configuration was tested); actual value for business decisions.

**To keep in mind:** cost and reasoning use came in below expectations and should not be extrapolated to longer tasks. Three repeats show variation within a task only.
